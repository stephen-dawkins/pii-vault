START HERE: PII Vault

Use ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude, or any AI tool on files with student data, without the AI ever seeing the real names.

1. Install 2. Open it the first time 3. Welcome screen 4. Your PII Vault folder 5. Using it 6. Updates Help

Don't have it yet? Download PII Vault for Mac (PII-Vault.dmg).

Setup takes about five minutes. You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 13 Ventura or later. Everything runs on your Mac, and nothing is sent anywhere except the protected copies you give your AI tool.

1 Drag PII Vault into Applications

Double-click PII-Vault.dmg (it's probably in your Downloads folder). A window opens with the PII Vault icon and an Applications folder. Drag the icon onto the Applications folder.

Drag the PII Vault icon onto the Applications folder.

Important: always open PII Vault from your Applications folder, not from this window. It can only update itself when it lives in Applications.

2 Open it the first time (the "Open Anyway" step)

PII Vault was made at Ole Miss and isn't sold through Apple's App Store, so the first time you open it your Mac shows a warning. That's expected. You'll allow it once, and you won't see the warning again.

a. Open PII Vault and click Done

Open your Applications folder and double-click PII Vault. Your Mac says it can't verify the app. Click Done (on older Macs it says OK or Cancel). Don't click Move to Trash.

Click Done. The wording can differ a little depending on your macOS version.

b. Go to Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway

  1. Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner of your screen, then System Settings.
  2. Click Privacy & Security in the list on the left.
  3. Scroll all the way down to the Security section. You'll see a line that says PII Vault was blocked.
  4. Click Open Anyway. This button only appears for about an hour after step a, so if you don't see it, open PII Vault again and come back.

System Settings > Privacy & Security, scrolled down to Security.

c. Confirm

Your Mac asks one more time. Click Open Anyway, then type your Mac password (or use Touch ID) if it asks. PII Vault opens.

Click Open Anyway. You only do this once.

That's the only awkward part. From now on PII Vault opens normally, and updates install from inside the app.

3 The welcome screen

  1. Pick the AI tool you use (ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini, Claude, or Other). This only changes the Open button. Protecting and restoring work the same with any tool.
  2. Your PII Vault folder is made for you. You don't choose where anything goes (more on the folder below).
  3. Recommended: better name finding. PII Vault checks for Ollama, a free app that runs a small, private name-finding model on your Mac. If you don't have it, click Download Ollama, drag it to Applications, and open it. PII Vault notices by itself, then click Download Model. You can skip this and do it later in Settings.

Click Get Started and you're done.

Your Mac may ask whether PII Vault can use your Downloads folder. Click Allow. That lets PII Vault put the real data back into files you download from your AI tool without you dragging them in.

4 Your PII Vault folder

Everything lives in one folder called PII Vault in your home folder (the one with the house icon and your name, under Macintosh HD > Users). It's kept out of Documents and Desktop on purpose, because those are often synced to iCloud, and restored student data should never be copied to the cloud.

Tip: drag the PII Vault folder into the Finder sidebar so it's always one click away.

For AI > ProtectedProtected copies of your files, with names, emails, and IDs swapped for tokens like PERSON_0001. These are the only files you give to an AI tool.
For AI > AI OutputOnly for AI tools that can save files on your Mac, like Claude's Cowork mode. Give that tool the For AI folder, never the whole PII Vault folder. Anything it saves here is restored automatically.
RestoredYour finished files with the real data put back. Private. Never upload anything from here to an AI tool.

Your original files are never moved or changed. If a folder ever goes missing, PII Vault makes it again the next time it opens, and Settings > Folders > Repair fixes it on the spot.

Already used an older version? PII Vault moves your old Documents > PII Vault folder into the new spot by itself and shows you a short note when it does. If you connected the old For AI folder to Claude's Cowork mode, connect the new one instead.

5 Using it

  1. Drop a file onto PII Vault: a spreadsheet, Word document, PDF, or text file. You can also paste text.
  2. Check what it found. For spreadsheets, each column gets an action (Tokenize, Year only, and so on). If your spreadsheet has report info above the table, like course title, term, and CRN, PII Vault finds where the real table starts and asks whether to remove that info (the default) or keep it with any names swapped for tokens. Tabs with no table, like assignment instructions, can be included as text or left out.
  3. Type what the AI should do, then click Protect Files.
  4. Click Copy Files and paste into your AI tool, then Copy Prompt and paste. Send.
  5. Download the AI's answer. PII Vault spots it in your Downloads folder and puts the real data back by itself. The finished file goes in PII Vault > Restored. You can also drag the file onto PII Vault, or use Paste the AI's answer if the AI only shows text.
Never upload anything from the Restored folder to an AI tool. Those files have the real names in them.

6 Updates

PII Vault checks for a new version once a day when it opens. When there is one, you'll see Update to 0.x at the bottom of the sidebar. Open Settings > Updates and click Install Update. PII Vault downloads the new version, checks it, swaps it in, and reopens. Your folders, settings, and jobs stay as they are.

Settings > Updates

If you have version 0.3 or older, it can't update itself yet: download the newest installer from the link you got PII Vault from and drag it into Applications again, choosing Replace. Your files and settings are kept. After that, updates happen inside the app.

Help

I don't see Open Anyway

Open PII Vault from Applications again, click Done, and go straight back to System Settings > Privacy & Security. The button only shows for about an hour after a blocked launch.

A red bar says PII Vault can't use a folder

Click Repair. If it comes back, click Open Settings, go to Files and Folders, make sure PII Vault is switched on, then click Repair again.

My AI's file didn't get restored

Make sure you allowed PII Vault to use Downloads (System Settings > Privacy & Security > Files and Folders). Or just drag the downloaded file onto PII Vault; that always works.

"PII Vault can't replace itself" when updating

PII Vault is running from the installer or your Downloads folder. Drag it into Applications, open it from there, and click Install Update again.
